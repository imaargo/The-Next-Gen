Why PT7

Key Features

Engineered for highway durability, wet safety, and everyday efficiency – every detail of the PT7 is built around a balanced, confident drive.

Reduced Road Noise

5-pitch sequencing technology breaks up air-pumping frequencies between tread blocks for a quieter highway ride.

Symmetrical Tread Pattern

Balanced rib design ensures even pressure distribution for uniform wear and extended tyre life across all seasons.

Wetland Safety

Four-channel drainage system rapidly evacuates water to maintain confident wet braking and hydroplaning resistance.

Lightweight Off-Road Design

Reinforced sidewall construction handles gravel shoulders and light trail duties without adding unsprung weight.

Tough & Long-lasting

Deep 8mm tread depth with wear-resistant compound delivers exceptional mileage even under heavy highway use.

Driving Control

Optimised contact patch shape delivers stable straight-line tracking and confident lane-change responsiveness.
