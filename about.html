Officer South, Victoria

Empowering Australian Mobility

THE NEXT GEN VIC PTY LTD is the importer and distributor of world-class tyre brands Praxent and Botrian. Based in Officer South, Victoria, we bridge the gap between advanced international tyre manufacturing and the rigorous demands of Australian drivers.

Our executive and technical advisory team brings over 34 years of hands-on automotive expertise to ensure every tyre model meets and exceeds Australian Design Rules (ADR) and international safety benchmarks.

Who We Are

We do not just distribute tyres. We provide end-to-end technical consultation, localised quality assurance, and dedicated distributor support. Our mission is to deliver tyres that offer uncompromising safety, long-lasting durability, and superior driving comfort across every Australian road condition.

From high-speed interstate highway touring to rough regional tarmac and extreme outback corrugated trails, Australian driving conditions demand exceptional resilience. Every Praxent tyre is engineered with purpose.
