Why PEV80

Key Features

Purpose-built for electric mobility – every detail of the PEV80 is engineered around instant torque, extended range, and cabin silence.

Comfortable and Quiet

Seagull-wing bionic pitch sequence keeps cabin noise at or below 68dB even on coarse-chip surfaces, matching premium luxury-car NVH standards.

Steady and Smooth

Low centre-of-gravity tread layout delivers stable, vibration-free ride quality at highway speeds.

Durable and Long-lasting

Silica-plus-functional-polymer compound resists chunking and chipping for extended tread life under heavy EV torque loads.

Intermittent Closed Grooves

EV-specific closed shoulder grooves reduce air-column resonance for ultra-quiet operation.

New SBR Formula

Solution-polymerised SBR compound reduces rolling resistance to maximise battery range per charge.

Optimised Tread Pattern

Directional rib layout improves straight-line stability and reduces uneven wear from regenerative braking.
