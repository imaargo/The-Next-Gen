Why BC25

Key Features

Engineered for quiet comfort, fuel economy, and wet-road confidence – every detail of the BC25 is built around a smoother, more efficient drive.

Ultra Low Noise

Pitch sequence optimization and multi-zone tread block design minimize pattern noise for a whisper-quiet ride.

Efficient Fuel Consumption

Optimized bead structure and lightweight carcass reduce energy loss, delivering outstanding fuel economy.

Outstanding Handling

High-stiffness shoulder blocks and precision-engineered crown radius ensure responsive, predictable cornering.

Superior Wet Performance

Four wide circumferential grooves and dense siping rapidly evacuate water, boosting wet braking and hydroplaning resistance.

All Season Durability

Advanced tread compound with high-dispersion silica balances wear resistance and grip across diverse road conditions.
