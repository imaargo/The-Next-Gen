Why BU78

Key Features

Engineered for maximum grip, precise handling, and high-speed stability – every detail of the BU78 is built for the ultimate performance driving experience.

Responsive Handling

Reinforced sidewall construction and optimized contact patch deliver razor-sharp steering precision and immediate directional response.

Superior Wet Braking

High-silica compound and wide circumferential grooves rapidly evacuate water, significantly reducing wet braking distance.

Enhanced Fuel Efficiency

Low rolling resistance compound and optimized casing geometry minimize energy loss, contributing to reduced fuel consumption.

High-Speed Stability

Advanced belt package and rigid crown structure maintain precise tread contact and vehicle composure at sustained high-speed driving.

All-Season Traction

Multi-functional tread compound balances dry grip, wet adhesion, and cold-weather flexibility for confident year-round performance.
